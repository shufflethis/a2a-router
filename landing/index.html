    06 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

    Before you start.

    Is this already a verified marketplace?

    No. Today you can use the order passport and agent interfaces. Factory audits, shared reputation, buyer protection, and payments are future stages.

    What does the hash chain prove?

    It links the brief and recorded events using SHA-256. It detects inconsistent changes within an export. Someone who recalculates the entire passport can create a different history. It does not prove identity, truth, server-side immutability, or payment. Keep exported snapshots for comparison.

    Where is my data stored?

    With JavaScript, preparation stays local. Saved orders are in this browser's localStorage and are not automatically sent to us. Without JavaScript, the form sends the brief to our API, which creates a draft without saving an order. Clearing browser data removes local orders. Export important passports. The bridge sends targeted calls to your chosen endpoints. Agent Tracking (agenttracking.co) records page paths, referral domains, utm_source when present, tool names, duration and status, and clicks on marked actions. Its embedded script does not use cookies or send form values or order contents. Connecting to the service involves technical connection data. Tracking service privacy policy.

    How do I move from a brief to manufacturing?

    Download the brief and send it to your chosen supplier. You can optionally use the Vibe Manufacturing request form . Review recipients and data use before submitting there. Your passport is not transferred automatically.

    Is the open Trust-Pledge still available?

    Yes. The signed Trust-Pledge and signed Agent Card remain available as existing, self-attested bridge metadata. They describe the earlier bridge MVP. The current product status is published separately.
